# Adventure Backpack

A backpack you wear, with two fluid tanks, a crafting grid and 76 looks, many of them with an ability of their own. For Minecraft 1.7.10 with Forge.

> Screenshot: a player wearing a backpack, seen from behind, standing in a field. File: images/home-1.png

The backpack goes on your back, not in an armor slot, so it does not take inventory space from your gear. Open it with B from anywhere: you get 39 storage slots, two tool slots, two tanks with bucket slots, and a 3x3 crafting grid. Craft it into another type and it keeps everything inside.

Around the backpack the mod adds a hose to drink and move fluids, a crossbow with three modes, a steam jetpack, a copter pack, scuba gear, a flamethrower, a fuel station, a sleeping bag with a campfire you can cook on, an inflatable boat with a motor, and a few pieces of adventuring gear.

## What you get

- 76 backpacks. Most are craftable from a Standard backpack, and 20 of them do something while you wear them: night vision, fire resistance, milk from wheat, eggs, calm wolves, tame spiders to ride, burning punches, and so on.
- Two tanks per backpack, filled with buckets or with the hose. The hose also lets you drink from the tank.
- A Clockwork Crossbow loaded from Bolt Cartridges, with three modes: a magazine of six bolts with a held right click, a grappling hook that pulls you to a block, and a sniper shot with a bipod. A key changes the mode.
- A Steam Jetpack, powered by water and a furnace fuel, and a Copter Pack that burns liquid fuel from BuildCraft, Forestry, Railcraft or GregTech. Flying costs: a full tank gives a minute or two.
- Scuba Gear with two air tanks and a diving mask: ten minutes under water, filled at an Air Compressor you pump by hand or run on any furnace fuel.
- A Flamethrower: a pack on your back with two fuel tanks and a nozzle in your hand that throws flame in a cone. A Fuel Station fills it, the Copter Pack and the motor of the Motorized Inflatable Boat.
- A sleeping bag you deploy from a placed backpack or lay on the ground on its own. Sleep next to a campfire and you respawn there. You can cook on the campfire.
- Support for Baubles, Baubles Expanded, OpenBlocks graves and GraveStone.

## Getting started

1. Craft a Standard Adventure Backpack. See [Backpacks](Backpacks).
2. Hold it and right click in the air. The backpack window opens; press the Equip button.
3. Press B at any time to open the worn backpack.
4. Craft a hose, put water or milk in a tank, and try the three modes. See [Fluids and Hose](Fluids-and-Hose).
5. Surround the backpack with the items of the type you want in the crafting table. Everything inside stays. See [Backpacks](Backpacks).
