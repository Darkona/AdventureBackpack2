Sound credits of Adventure Backpack
===================================

Boat motor sounds (assets/adventurebackpack/sounds/boatMotor*.ogg). All three are released under Creative Commons 0 (public domain dedication), so no attribution is required; they are listed here anyway. Each was cut, converted to mono Ogg Vorbis (44.1 kHz) and faded at its ends for this mod.

  Use:     a 5 second stretch (30 s to 35.5 s of the original), joined end to start with a half second crossfade so it loops without a click.
