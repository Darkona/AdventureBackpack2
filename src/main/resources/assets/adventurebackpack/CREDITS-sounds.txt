Sound credits of Adventure Backpack
===================================

Boat motor sounds (assets/adventurebackpack/sounds/boatMotor*.ogg). All three are released under Creative Commons 0 (public domain dedication), so no attribution is required; they are listed here anyway. Each was cut, converted to mono Ogg Vorbis (44.1 kHz) and faded at its ends for this mod.

boatMotorLoop.ogg
  Title:   Slowboat Engine Reving
  Author:  FillSoko
  URL:     https://freesound.org/people/FillSoko/sounds/456083/
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  Use:     a 5 second stretch (30 s to 35.5 s of the original), joined end to start with a half second crossfade so it loops without a click.

boatMotorStop.ogg
  Title:   Lawnmower engine stop 0
  Author:  piotrkier
  URL:     https://freesound.org/people/piotrkier/sounds/700146/
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  Use:     the whole clip (1.5 s) with short fades.

boatMotorStart.ogg
  Title:   Lawnmower pulling cord 2
  Author:  piotrkier
  URL:     https://freesound.org/people/piotrkier/sounds/700142/
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  Use:     the whole clip (0.7 s) with short fades.

Flamethrower sounds. The roar of the flame is the mod's own work, released with the mod; the other flamethrower sounds are vanilla Minecraft sounds called by name (fire.ignite, random.fizz, random.click, mob.ghast.fireball), not shipped in the mod.

flamethrowerLoop.ogg
  Author:  made for Adventure Backpack (no recording used)
  Use:     3 seconds of synthesized noise (a low roar, a band of hiss and random crackles, each with a slow random swell), joined end to start with a 0.4 second crossfade so it loops without a click; mono Ogg Vorbis, 44.1 kHz.
